# huang_cheng_ppe_6class_3050 — Data Card

Detection dataset for personal protective equipment, YOLO format, 6 classes (`classes.txt`, same as `data.yaml`):

`0 Helmet, 1 NoHelmet, 2 NoVest, 3 Vest, 4 RegularHat, 5 Person`

`RegularHat` is an ordinary hat that is not a safety helmet (baseball cap, brimmed hat, bucket hat and similar).
The dataset replaces `css`, `ppe2286` and `ppe5` (deleted on 2026-11-24). Person moved from index 4 (ppe5) to 5.

## Origin

Compiled by the dataset owner from three kinds of images:

1. the `ppe2286` dataset, Huang M-L, Cheng Y., "Dataset of personal protective equipment", *Data in Brief* 2025,
   DOI 10.1016/j.dib.2025.111988,
2. images found on the internet,
3. images generated by AI (synthetic).

Local folder `data/huang_cheng_ppe_6class_3050/` (images and labels are not tracked in git, only this card).
Export report: `DATASET_EXPORT_REPORT.json`. The report names the source archive `Huang_Cheng_PPE6_Final_3074.zip`
(3,074 images), 24 were removed in this export, 3,050 kept.

## Exclusions

The export report lists 23 images excluded earlier (`previously_excluded_images`) and 24 excluded in this export
(`excluded_in_this_export`), each with a reason: repeated crop of the same scene, vest catalog crop, too dark, and
similar. The excluded images are not in the folder.

## Checks (2026-11-25)

- All 3,050 images and label files match the SHA-256 in the export report. No two images share the same bytes.
- Class order checked by eye on 3 random `train` images and 8 random `NoHelmet` crops: Helmet on hard hats,
  NoHelmet on bare heads, NoVest on torsos without a vest, Vest on reflective vests, RegularHat on caps and brimmed
  hats, Person on whole bodies. Small sample, not a full audit.

## Known limits

- `NoHelmet` is the rarest class (2,898 boxes), so its AP varies most.
- Helmet, NoHelmet and RegularHat boxes are small (median 0.3% to 0.5% of the image area), expect lower AP at small
  input sizes.
- Valid and test contain real photos and AI-generated images, so mAP on them does not measure accuracy on real
  camera footage.
